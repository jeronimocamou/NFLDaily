How to play

You're down 21–17 with the ball on your own 25. Drive for the winning touchdown.

  • Every play is a question like "Name a QB who started for the Browns since 1999". Start typing and pick a name.
  • A right answer is a pass. The obvious answers (the stars) are a 🟨 Check Down: 7 yards. Less obvious ones are a 🟩 Seam Route: 20 yards. The deepest cuts are 💣 Bomb: 40 yards. Each question shows how many answers fall in each tier.
  • A wrong answer is an incomplete pass. You get 30 seconds per play.
  • 4 downs to gain 10 yards. Reach the end zone to win.

After each play you'll see every right answer, grouped by tier. Leaderboards rank by points, then yards, then fewer plays; only same-day drives by signed-in players count.
