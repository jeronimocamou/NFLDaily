NFL Daily

    How to play

    Every day there are 5 rounds, each from a different NFL season (1999–2025). All stats are regular season only.

    • Better record — pick the team with the better record.
    • Odd one out — find the team that doesn't belong.
    • Guess the number — get close for partial credit.
    • Rank four — tap teams in order, best first. 25 points per correct spot.

    Each round is worth up to 100 points. A new puzzle drops at midnight.
