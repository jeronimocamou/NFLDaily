NFL Daily

    How to play

    Every day there are 5 rounds, each from a different NFL season (1999–2025). All stats are regular season only.

    • Head to head: which team had the better record, or which player had more?
    • Odd one out: find the team that doesn't belong.
    • Guess the number: get close for partial credit.
    • Rank four: tap in order, best first. Points for each correct spot.
    • Who am I?: name the player from a stat line. A miss gives you a hint and one more try for 50.

    Each round is worth up to 100 points. A new puzzle drops at midnight. Missed a day? Play it from the calendar, but only same-day games count toward streaks and leaderboards.
